TINDA POS Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 24, 2026

At TINDA POS Web (accessible from https://tindapos-web.pages.dev), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and retail users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use, safeguard, and disclose it in accordance with applicable data privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google publisher policies.

1. Offline-First Local Storage Architecture

TINDA POS Web is designed with an offline-first architecture. Your store products, local transactions, receipts, customer debt ledger, and configuration are saved securely in your browser's private IndexedDB storage (Dexie.js). We do not sell, rent, or monetize your store transaction records.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements and provide a free-tier point of sale experience for sari-sari store owners and retail micro-merchants.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through www.aboutads.info.

3. European Regulations & Consent Management (GDPR/EEA/UK/Switzerland)

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we have adopted Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). When you access our services from these regions, you will be presented with a consent banner allowing you to consent to or manage advertising preferences, cookies, and data processing choices.

4. Rewarded Video Ads & Pro Time-Bank System

To access advanced features (such as real-time DTI SRP Suggested Retail Price cloud sync, comprehensive Z-reading reports, and automated shelf-life expiration alerts) without financial payments, users may optionally watch short sponsored rewarded ads. Watching an ad records an anonymized timestamp and duration token in your browser to grant temporary access. No sensitive retail credentials are transferred to ad providers.

5. Log Files and Web Analytics

Like most web applications hosted on modern edge content delivery networks (Cloudflare Pages), automated access logs may temporarily record internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, referring/exit pages, and operating systems to prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and ensure network uptime.
