## 📌 Executive Summary

Under Philippine law (**R.A. 7581 / Price Act**), the **Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)** periodically issues and enforces the **Suggested Retail Price (SRP) Bulletin** for **Basic Necessities and Prime Commodities (BNPC)**. 

### Why SRP Matters for Retailers & Sari-Sari Stores:
1. **Consumer Fair-Pricing Guarantee:** Protects Filipino consumers against unfair price gouging and artificial inflation.
2. **Mandatory Price Ceiling During Calamities:** Automatic 60-day **Price Freeze** is triggered whenever a municipality, province, or region is declared under a State of Calamity or Emergency.
3. **Retail Inventory Reference:** Serves as the official market benchmark for calculating wholesale margins, markup percentages, and selling prices in TINDA POS.
